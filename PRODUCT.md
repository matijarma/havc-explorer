# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Filmmakers, producers, researchers, journalists, and members of the public who need to inspect Croatian audiovisual public-funding decisions. They typically browse on a desktop while comparing projects, recipients, years, programmes, and source documents.

## Product Purpose

Sredstva turns HAVC's scattered public funding records into a searchable, filterable, verifiable registry. Success means users can understand funding patterns quickly, inspect a project's complete support history, and trace every figure back to the source material.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, rigorous, and direct. The interface should feel like a trustworthy public-data instrument with cultural character, not a corporate analytics product.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative metric-card grids, leaderboard framing, glossy data visualizations, gratuitous motion, and interactions that conceal provenance or exact values.

## Design Principles

1. Prioritize comparison and investigation over decoration.
2. Keep the registry dense, legible, and predictable for repeated use.
3. Use progressive disclosure: show the essential overview first and preserve full detail on demand.
4. Maintain a clear distinction between global registry facts and the user's active filtered scope.
5. Keep every important number explainable and connected to source evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, bilingual Croatian and English content, light and dark themes, reduced-motion compatibility, and data encodings that do not depend on color alone.
